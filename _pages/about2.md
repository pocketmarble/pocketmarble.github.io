Hey, I'm Austin 👋 welcome to my website! Born and raised on Long Island, currently living in Brooklyn.

I'm a computational scientist looking for roles where rigorous analysis meets real-world impact, particularly in oncology, computational biology, and real-world evidence.

My career spans a decade in the New York research community, working at the intersection of genomics, oncology, and machine learning. I started in applied math and biomedical engineering and grew into a scientist who can take a study the whole way, from the biological question, through the experiment and the data, to an analysis that answers it. Most of that work has been in cancer, with published research in hepatocellular carcinoma, melanoma, and immuno-oncology (see the [publications](https://pocketmarble.github.io/publications/) page for the whole list), and along the way I became the person labs counted on to turn dense multi-omic data into clear results. I'm currently finishing a Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences and Public Health at the Instituto de Salud Carlos III in Madrid, via the Icahn School of Medicine in NYC.

Check out the navigation bar above to find my [projects](https://pocketmarble.github.io/projects/) and [resume](https://pocketmarble.github.io/cv/). Find my LinkedIn [here](https://www.linkedin.com/in/austin-mead0ws/).
